Near-Infrared Colorization

I used to fly at night with the help of a MaxVis EVS camera mounted to the wing of my Cirrus SR22. Now that I'm building a Velocity, I've been experimenting with a FLIR Hadron 640R camera attached to the wing's tie-down loop and streaming to the cockpit over Wi-Fi. To improve visibility, I colorize the NIR feed in real time with Pix2Pix, a generative adversarial network. I trained it on the EPFL RGB-NIR dataset; it runs at 50 fps and works surprisingly well.
