CoRL 2026 — Every Talk, Indexed by AI

The Conference on Robot Learning 2026 (Austin, Texas, Nov 9–12) streamed its whole main program in four long recordings. These are broken into individual presentations: keynotes, orals, spotlights, sponsor talks and the debate panel. Each entry has a short summary, key takeaways and a player that starts and stops on that talk.

Summaries are written from the livestream captions and the paper abstracts. Each paper links to arXiv and its project page where available, and to OpenReview. Recordings © CoRL 2026.
