Renato Salas, PhD

Surreal Vision Ltd

I co-founded Surreal Vision Ltd (2014–2015) with Dr. Richard Newcombe (now at Meta) and Steven Lovegrove (now at Farm NG). Our goal was to provide superhuman visual perception for VR & AR.

Facebook Acquisition

We were fortunate to be acquired by Facebook in 2015 and join the Oculus Research team in Redmond, WA, where we kept pushing the boundaries of enabling real-time scene understanding on VR headsets. It was a remarkable outcome for a company only eight months old. We never raised venture capital, although we did win a UK government grant.

Fun fact: Our first customer was Meta, an early pioneer of AR headsets (now defunct). Little did we know Facebook would later rename itself Meta. It was exhilarating to work with the original Meta at its headquarters in the heart of Silicon Valley, and I remain thankful to its then-CEO, Meron Gribetz, for supporting our young startup.
