Renato Salas, PhD

At a Glance

Robotics Scientist / Aviator / Angel Investor

Renato is a scientist and entrepreneur in camera-based navigation systems. He previously co-founded Surreal Vision Ltd (VR perception, acquired by Facebook) and Vtrus Inc (indoor drones for inspection), and was named one of MIT Technology Review's Innovators Under 35.

He holds an instrument-rated pilot license and builds an experimental aircraft in his free time.

He makes occasional angel investments in robotics and AI, typically alongside a lead VC.
