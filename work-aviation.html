Renato Salas, PhD

Aviation

I grew up next to a small airfield (ICAO: SPTQ) high in the mountains of Toquepala, Peru, and I have been obsessed with flying machines ever since. That obsession led me to build drones, earn my pilot's license and fly the Cirrus SR22 and Icon A5. Most recently I've been building a Velocity experimental aircraft, and to prepare I took the composite materials and mold-making courses at DarkAero.
